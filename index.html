Research question

How do AI product announcements, infrastructure investment and partnership strategies relate to financial performance and investor expectations?

Interpretation rule: Compare dated AI events with earnings and market performance, but do not claim causation based on price movements alone.

Financial interpretation checklist

  • Review earnings and SEC disclosures, not just stock returns.
  • Compare revenue growth with AI product launch and infrastructure announcement dates.
  • Separate fiscal-year and fiscal-quarter values.
  • Avoid attributing all company revenue to AI; AI-specific segment revenues are not always disclosed.

Data pipeline

  1. Collector reads SEC XBRL, market history and official RSS feeds.
  2. Normalize records with company, fiscal period and source metadata.
  3. Validate response and retain last successful data on errors.
  4. GitHub Actions schedules updates and commits snapshot.json.
  5. This HTML fetches the current published JSON and renders charts and headlines.

AI agents and RAG: implementation boundary

The collector is automated ETL, not an autonomous AI agent. This standalone HTML does not perform live LLM inference or vector retrieval. To claim RAG implementation, add document parsing, embeddings/vector indexing, retrieval, a model-generated response, and verifiable citations in a hosted service.

Project strengths: interactive financial visualization, sourced evidence, automatic scheduled updates once deployed, and clear limitations.
